# Man Vs Machine

**Man Vs Machine** is a fast-paced, 2D endless survival game inspired by Plants vs. Zombies, built in [Godot](https://godotengine.org). \

The goal is simple. Pick your units, defend the field, and see how long you can survive against the machines! 

---

## Main Features:

* **Unit Selectcion:** Build your loadout strategically! Each card has a "strength" value, and you can't go over the maximum! \
* **Custom Animations:** Every animation is handdrawn in Pixelorama with love to bring the game to life! \
* **Shop:** After a run, you can visit the shop to purchase stat upgrades which make your runs easier! \
* **Original Soundtrack:** A handmade soundtrack made in FL Studio to make the experience even more immersive! \
* **Endless Wave System:** Go for as long as you can as the waves get harder and harder! \
